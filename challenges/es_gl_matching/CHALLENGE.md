# Spanish clues to Galician descriptors (folio matching)  [verbatim description]

Overview: Two writers can describe the same specialized object without using the same words or even the same kind of evidence. One passage may define its form, while another explains its use, exceptions, or distinction from nearby concepts. In each folio, match short Spanish clues to Galician descriptors using the content of both passages. The alternatives concern closely related concepts, and each descriptor belongs to exactly one clue. 8, 10, or 12 items per folio, one assignment per folio.

Headwords, direct bilingual label pairs, complete classification paths, and original record identifiers are absent from the prediction inputs. The paired passages come from different descriptive views, so literal translation of one full passage does not supply its counterpart. Available evidence varies across folios.

Novelty: complete one-to-one assignment among near neighbors from nonparallel evidence; folio sizes vary; the participant must report confidence for every proposed link. A nearest-neighbor ranking alone does not resolve within-folio competition or supply well-supported confidence. Primary modeling work: learn cross-language passage representations or pairwise comparison scores, then reconcile those scores across each folio. Fine-tuning a multilingual encoder or training a compact reranker are natural approaches.

Released data: 1,000 training folios and 150 evaluation folios, 9,998 training targets and 1,500 evaluation targets. Each folio has the same number of Spanish clues and Galician descriptors: 8, 10 or 12. A clue has target_id and text; a descriptor has descriptor_id and text. Arrays independently shuffled; IDs opaque and local to their folio.
- folios.jsonl: folio_id, spanish_clues, galician_descriptors (one JSON object per line).
- train.csv: target_id,folio_id,folio_byte_anchor,folio_byte_span,evidence_band.
- train_targets.csv: target_id,prediction (JSON with the correct descriptor_id and confidence 1.0).
- test.csv: same columns as train.csv without labels. sample_submission.csv: legal format examples.
Byte anchor/span are transport offsets, not semantic inputs. evidence_band is short, mixed or extended. Training and evaluation have separate concept families; concepts may occur in multiple folios within a partition, with independently presented evidence.

Submission: exactly one row per evaluation target_id, columns target_id,prediction only. prediction is a JSON object with exactly descriptor_id and confidence. The ID must name a descriptor from the target's own folio. confidence is a finite number in [1/n, 1] (n = folio size), the estimated probability that the selected descriptor is correct; the remaining probability is treated as equal mass over the other n-1 descriptors. Each descriptor should be assigned to one clue in its folio. Reusing a descriptor is a scoreable matching error. Malformed prediction, out-of-range confidence, blank or cross-folio descriptor ID gives zero on that target. Wrong header, duplicate target ID, missing or extra target ID invalidates the submission.

Evaluation: in folio f, a_f = fraction of correct assignments. Split the public spanish_clues array into first and last halves; their correct fractions are l_f and r_f. Confidence uses a multiclass Brier skill score: probability c on the chosen descriptor and (1-c)/(n-1) on each other descriptor; B = sum of squared differences to the one-hot correct answer; per-target skill = max(0, 1 - B/(1-1/n)); k_f = mean skill over the folio. Folio score = 0.60 a_f + 0.20 sqrt(l_f r_f) + 0.20 k_f. Folio scores averaged within each evidence_band; the arithmetic mean of the present band scores x100, clipped to [0.01, 100].

Required modeling and compute: task-trained neural model on one NVIDIA A10G (24 GB), full run incl. training/fine-tuning and evaluation inference within 90 minutes. The GPU-trained component must materially generate the scored comparison matrix or descriptor assignments. NOT eligible as primary: CPU-only classical ML, TF-IDF, BM25, bag-of-words, n-grams, edit distance, fixed-embedding nearest-neighbor lookup, hand-written dictionaries, rule-based or heuristic candidate pipelines; a token GPU training step does not make them eligible. CPU parsing, batching and one-to-one decoding of learned GPU scores are permitted complements. Do not consult external answer lists or reconstruct links from original identifiers; predictions must come from the released inputs and eligible model weights.
