== 7. PHYSICS LINK: IS GRF PREDICTABLE FROM COM ACCELERATION?
==============================================================================
Newton: sum(F_ext) = m*a_com, and GRF/(m*g) = a_com/g + gravity term.
With markers only on the lower body we can only approximate the COM, but the
correlation tells us how much of the force is directly kinematic.
  corr(force_x, pelvis_acc_x) = +0.5182
  corr(force_x, pelvis_acc_y) = +0.1971
  corr(force_x, pelvis_acc_z) = -0.0187
  -> force_x best matched by pelvis_acc_x (+0.5182)
  corr(force_y, pelvis_acc_x) = +0.0468
  corr(force_y, pelvis_acc_y) = +0.3111
  corr(force_y, pelvis_acc_z) = +0.0567
  -> force_y best matched by pelvis_acc_y (+0.3111)
  corr(force_z, pelvis_acc_x) = -0.0097
  corr(force_z, pelvis_acc_y) = +0.0015
